01

Overview

Spendex is a crypto payments platform built for individuals and small-to-medium businesses. It lets users send, receive, and spend crypto in the real world , not as speculation, but as functional, everyday utility.

I joined as CMO and Content Lead with a mandate to build the growth infrastructure from scratch: define how the product is positioned, build the acquisition engine, and make a technically complex product legible to ordinary users. This wasn't a campaign brief. It was full ownership of the growth layer.

02

The Problem

Crypto payments products face a trust and comprehension gap that most marketing never closes. The product works , but the average person doesn't believe it, doesn't understand it, or doesn't see why it matters to them.

Spendex had a working product and real infrastructure but no coherent public presence. The challenges were layered:

  • No brand voice or messaging framework , the product existed, but there was no clear story for why it mattered or who it was for
  • Zero organic discovery , no SEO structure, no search presence, no inbound content to pull in high-intent users
  • Complex product, simple audience , most target users (SMB owners, freelancers, individuals) aren't crypto-native and needed a bridge
  • Competing with noise , the crypto space is full of vague promises; building trust required showing real utility, not hype
03

The Approach

I started with positioning before touching any distribution channel. A clear answer to "who is this for and why should they care" is the prerequisite for everything else , content, SEO, ads, onboarding copy. Without it, output is just noise.

Positioning First

Mapped Spendex's core user segments , freelancers receiving cross-border payments, SMBs wanting faster settlement, crypto holders who actually want to spend. Built a messaging framework tailored to each.

Utility Over Hype

Every piece of content was anchored in use-case: what problem does this solve, for whom, in what situation. No vague promises. Concrete, relatable scenarios that made the product's value immediate.

Search as an Acquisition Channel

Built the SEO website architecture around high-intent search behavior , people actively looking for crypto payment solutions for their business. Organic beats paid for building durable trust.

Video as a Trust Accelerator

Scripted and produced video ad content designed to compress the trust-building timeline , showing real product flows, real use cases, and real outcomes in 30–60 seconds.

04

What I Did

My scope covered three core workstreams running concurrently , this wasn't sequential work. Positioning, content, and SEO informed each other in real time.

  • Brand Messaging & Voice , defined Spendex's positioning, tone, and narrative framework. Wrote core homepage copy, product descriptions, and onboarding language
  • SEO Website Architecture , designed and built an SEO-optimized site structure targeting high-intent queries around crypto payments, merchant adoption, and cross-border transactions
  • Social Content Operations , owned all organic social channels. Produced a consistent content cadence mixing product education, use-case storytelling, and social proof
  • Video Ad Scripts & Production Direction , wrote, structured, and produced video ad content translating Spendex's utility into short-form narratives that convert skeptics into sign-ups
  • GTM Iteration , tracked performance across every channel, identified what was converting and what wasn't, and iterated messaging based on data
05

Execution

The critical execution challenge was building trust with two very different audiences simultaneously , individual users who want simplicity and security, and SMB owners who want reliability and low friction.

I structured content and acquisition into two distinct tracks that shared brand tone but diverged in message, channel, and conversion trigger. The individual-user track leaned into simplicity, speed, and empowerment. The merchant track leaned into operational reliability, cost reduction, and settlement speed.

Key Execution Insight

The biggest conversion lever wasn't explaining blockchain , it was removing the need to. Users didn't need to understand the infrastructure. They needed to see a recognizable problem (slow transfers, high fees, limited payment options) solved in a way they already understood. We led with the outcome, not the mechanism.

  • Published a structured onboarding content series that reduced conceptual friction and accelerated time-to-first-action
  • Designed the SEO site to capture intent at three stages: problem-aware, solution-aware, and product-aware users
  • Produced video ad variants targeting both individual users and merchant owners with distinct value propositions
  • Built a content measurement system tracking content-attributed signups, not just engagement metrics

Key Insight

The Real Unlock

Most crypto products market to the already-converted. The real growth surface is the person who has heard of crypto but never seen a reason to use it. Winning that audience requires meeting them in their existing frame of reference , payments, fees, transfer speeds , not in yours. The product's job is to solve a real problem. Marketing's job is to make that solution feel obvious and safe. That's what we built.

08

What This Project Shows

  • Cross-functional ownership , I operated across product messaging, content, SEO, and video production simultaneously, not in sequence
  • GTM systems thinking , I didn't run individual campaigns; I built a durable growth infrastructure the team could continue operating independently
  • High-trust market execution , growing a live, real-money product demands a higher standard of clarity and credibility than most marketing contexts. We met it
  • Dual-audience strategy , managing different acquisition motions for consumers and SMBs concurrently without diluting either message
  • Content-to-conversion discipline , measuring content by signups and merchant onboarding, not vanity metrics
